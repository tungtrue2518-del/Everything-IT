Overview

Problems were found when users called the help desk: a full disk, an expired certificate, a failed backup.

The challenge

Catch the common failures early without paying for a large monitoring platform.

What I did

  1. Listed the ten incidents that caused the most tickets last year.
  2. Wrote small Python checks for each one: disk space, certificate expiry, backup status, license counts.
  3. Sent results to Grafana dashboards and posted alerts to a Teams channel.
  4. Tuned thresholds to avoid alert fatigue.

Results

incident tickets
-40%
automated checks
10

Lessons learned

Alert on what needs action, not on everything. A quiet channel gets read.
